1. Service Seeker Account:

  • Sign up from the "Post a Task" option, with a welcome page showing high-level company information
  • Choose account type: Personal or Business
  • Register with mobile number as the account ID, verified by OTP
  • Create password; change password and forgot password
  • Enter first name(s) and last name
  • Enter location: District, Thana, Ward/Village, Post code, Road name/Bari
  • Add special instructions (optional)
  • Submit and receive an instant success pop-up, then post tasks straight away
  • Update profile: mobile number, payment options, location
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • All fields are mandatory except Special instructions.
  • Mobile number must be registered under the user's NID. Warning shown: "Mobile number has to be registered under your NID". How this is checked is TBC.
  • Success message: "Congratulations! Your account has been created successfully. You are all set to post task"
  • Update message: "Your details have been updated successfully"
  • Password rules are "standard" here and "strong" under Security; one final policy is TBC.
  • TBC: extra Business account fields (company name, trade licence, BIN); age check for Seekers; OTP expiry and retry limits.

2. TukTaker Account:

  • 18+ eligibility check (Yes/No dropdown)
  • Personal account type only
  • Register with mobile number (account number) and OTP; create password; change and forgot password
  • Capture NID front photo, NID back photo and an app-directed selfie
  • Enter NID number
  • First name(s), last name and date of birth auto-populated from the NID image
  • Enter gender and address as per NID
  • Enter current address: District, Thana/Upozila, Address 1, Address 2
  • Enter work location: District, Thana, Ward/Village, Post code, Road name/Bari
  • Submit and enter Pending verification
  • Admin manually verifies NID and picture; account becomes Verified and shows a "V" with tick
  • SMS/Email notification when verified
  • Verified TukTaker can browse suitable tasks and bid
  • Update profile: mobile number, payment options, location
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • Answering "No" to 18+ disables all next steps. Message: "Sorry, you need to be minimum 18 years old to work with us"
  • Date of birth must indicate 18 years or older, checked at account creation. Message: "Opps! Look like you are entering a date of birth indicating you are not 18 years yet."
  • All fields are mandatory.
  • Submit message: "Congratulations! Your account has been created successfully. Please stay tuned while we verify your account"
  • Verified message: "Congratulations! Your account has been verified. You are ready to start browsing suitable tasks"
  • Only Verified TukTakers can search tasks and bid.
  • NID capture method, NID number and NID address handling to be discussed with the developer.
  • Fingerprint collection is not in MVP (TBC).
  • TBC: rejection and re-submission flow, verification time target, which profile changes need re-verification.

3. Task Categories:

  • Utilities: Electrical, Gas, Water, Internet, Cable TV
  • Cleaning: Domestic, Commercial, Industrial
  • Small Construction: Painting, Tiling, Window/Grill, Mason, Carpenter, Other
  • Moving: House, Shop, Factory, Warehouse, Office, Educational institute
  • Delivery (goods, documents)
  • Tutor
  • Catering
  • On-demand Patient Care Assistance (PCA)
  • Personal Assistant
  • Other (any task type)
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • Delivery requires the TukTaker to be 18+ and the task must include pick-up and drop-off addresses.
  • "Other" needs search optimised for keyword matching.
  • Personal Assistant description must be worded carefully to avoid compliance issues.
  • An emergency PCA service with hotline may be added in future.
  • Not in MVP (TBC): Car/truck rental, Driver hire, Maid, Cloth alteration, Nursing, Short-time apartment service.

4. Task Posting (Service Seeker Flow):

  • Login
  • Select category and sub-category
  • Describe the task in detail
  • Upload photo or measurement where applicable
  • Select date and time
  • Select location: District, Thana, Ward/Village, Post code, Road name/Bari
  • Answer pet question (Cat / Dog)
  • Set budget
  • Select payment option (bKash, Nagad)
  • Review task summary: description, date, time, location, budget, payment option
  • Confirm and post
  • Review bids and give a counter offer
  • Accept offer and pay
  • Accept task completion; payment is released from the vault
  • Rate the TukTaker
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • All steps are mandatory.
  • Pet message: "Dog must be leashed and supervised by owner while the task is being completed"
  • Payment goes through the mobile-payment gateway and is held in escrow until the Seeker accepts completion.
  • Seeker supplies spare parts for all works.
  • Task photos are kept for 3 months.
  • A Seeker can cancel free of charge before bidding closes (see Cancellation).
  • TBC: when bidding closes, how the budget works against bids, counter-offer limits, task editing after posting, auto-release if the Seeker never accepts.

5. Bidding & Task Execution (TukTaker Flow):

  • Login
  • Set location
  • Search tasks
  • Offer a bid
  • Review counter offer
  • Accept task
  • Notify arrival ("I have arrived")
  • Confirm task completed
  • Confirm payment received
  • Rate the Service Seeker
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • All steps are mandatory.
  • Only Verified TukTakers can bid.
  • TukTaker pays own communication costs to and from the site.
  • A cancelled task is re-opened for bidding automatically.
  • Arrival notice decides whether the Seeker's call-out fee applies.
  • TukTakers are self-employed contractors paid on commission, not employees.
  • TBC: search filters and radius, editing or withdrawing a bid, "Set app to ready mode" (no description in the sheet), no-show handling.

6. Payments, Escrow & Payouts:

  • Payment methods: bKash, Nagad, Bangla QR
  • Seeker pays after accepting an offer; money is held in escrow (vault)
  • Payment released to the TukTaker after the Seeker accepts completion
  • TukTaker earnings accumulate at account level
  • TukTaker redeems earnings to bKash, Nagad or Bangla QR
  • Optional tip from Seeker to TukTaker
  • Company receiving accounts: bank, bKash, Nagad, Bangla QR through the e-commerce gateway

Conditions / Business Rules:
  • Debit/Credit card is not in MVP (TBC).
  • Redeem amount: minimum 500 BDT, maximum 10,000 BDT; charge of 5 BDT per redemption; paid out through SSL Commerz.
  • Tips are optional and must be positive (100 accepted, -100 rejected); no commission on tips.
  • Release message: "Payment has been released."
  • Tip message: "Well done! Keep up the good work. You have received <tips_amount> taka tips"
  • Goodwill credits are not redeemable as cash.
  • TBC: whether the 10,000 BDT cap is per transaction, day or month; failed or double payments; payout wallet nomination point; Bangla QR missing from task-posting payment options.
  • Payout diagram lists a bank account as a destination; the sheet lists only bKash, Nagad and Bangla QR for redemption (TBC).

7. Commission & VAT:

  • Start with a flat commission on TukTaker earnings
  • Later introduce tiers: Silver, Gold, Platinum
  • Tier based on tasks completed in a period, total task value and rating
  • 15% VAT on the commission
  • VAT accumulated per service for the year-end tax return
  • Platform keeps 50% of cancellation fees as commission
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • No commission on tips.
  • Conflict in the sheet: flat 20% with tiers 20/18/16 (Split payment) versus flat 17% with tiers 17/15/13 (Our charge to TukTakers). Founders must choose one.
  • Flat rate may be adjusted as the platform grows (percentage TBC).
  • VAT applies to the commission, not the full task value.
  • A connection fee for Seekers and special offers are future options (TBC).
  • TBC: who bears the VAT, tier thresholds, downgrade rules.

8. Cancellation:

  • TukTaker may cancel within 2 hours of accepting the task
  • Penalty is deducted from the TukTaker's next service payment
  • Cancelled task re-opens for bidding and the Seeker is notified
  • Seeker may cancel before bidding closes at no cost
  • Seeker cancelling after bidding closes is charged a fee
  • Seeker cancelling after the TukTaker arrives is charged a call-out fee
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • Seeker fee after bidding closes: 10% of task value.
  • Call-out fee: up to 250 BDT, deducted through escrow to cover the platform fee and the TukTaker's time. Flat versus percentage to be reviewed.
  • TukTaker message: "Cancelling a task impacts your rating. Next time try to accommodate as best as you can"
  • Penalty message: "Penalty <penalty_amount> from last service has been deducted from this service fee"
  • Re-open message: "Your task has been re-opened for bidding. Please keep an eye"
  • Seeker messages: "Your task has been cancelled"; "Call out fee <cancellation_amount> taka has been deducted from your service charges for this task"
  • TBC: penalty amount, TukTaker cancellation after 2 hours, how the 10% and call-out fee combine.

9. Refunds & Dispute Resolution:

  • Back-end field to refund an agreed amount to the account level (TukTaker and Seeker)
  • Refund visible in the billing dashboard
  • Admin grants goodwill credit to a TukTaker account
  • Admin grants goodwill credit to a Seeker account
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • 5% of the task value is deducted as an admin fee to settle a refund.
  • Goodwill credits are not redeemable to bKash or Nagad; they offset service fees on the next task(s).
  • A maximum credit limit must be set to prevent fraud.
  • TukTaker credit message: "We have provided <credit_amount> taka credits to your account level. This <credit_amount> taka will be used towards your service fees for the next task with us"
  • Seeker credit message: "We have provided <credit_amount> taka credits to your account. You can use this <credit_amount> towards your service fee for the next task with us"
  • TBC: who bears the 5% fee, refund approval steps, how a dispute is raised, credit expiry.

10. Ratings & Testimonials:

  • Seeker rates TukTaker from 1 to 5 stars
  • TukTaker rates Seeker from 1 to 5 stars
  • Short testimonials from TukTakers and Seekers for the website and app
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • Rating is mandatory for both sides.
  • Cancelling a task affects the TukTaker's rating.
  • Rating feeds Silver/Gold/Platinum tiers.
  • Collect testimonials during the pilot where possible.
  • TBC: written comments, blind reveal, rating calculation, consequences of low ratings.

11. Invoicing & Billing Dashboards:

  • Invoice process kept ready for Seekers and TukTakers, monthly or quarterly (TBC)
  • Select a period and press "Generate Invoice"; user can print and save
  • Invoice shows company name, address, logo, trade licence number, customer account number, name, address
  • Invoice shows period, number of services, per-service category, sub-category and short description
  • Invoice shows total value, VAT amount, hotline and email
  • Billing dashboard at account level for each user, with an entry for every task
  • Totals tallied month-wise and year-wise
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • Invoice charge: 20 BDT per invoice.
  • Invoices are mainly for business customers' company records.
  • Value-added service charges are not in MVP (TBC).
  • TukTaker invoice wording is copied from the Seeker version and needs its own content (earnings, commission, VAT, net payout).
  • Refunds, penalties and credits should appear in the dashboard.

12. Admin, Reporting & System Maintenance:

  • User report: total TukTakers and Seekers for a date-time range
  • Revenue report: total revenue for a period
  • Task report: total tasks for a period
  • VAT report: total VAT for a period
  • Super User and General User roles
  • TukTaker verification, refunds and goodwill credits handled by admin
  • System partitioning where possible
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • Super Users can access and do things General Users cannot.
  • Major app and web updates are deployed at midnight to reduce downtime.
  • The admin sheet tab is empty, so admin screens are not specified yet.
  • "Set app to ready mode" has no description (TBC).

13. Security, Data Protection & Retention:

  • Protect customer data and business data
  • Secure repository
  • Regular backups to cloud plus an extra copy (external hard drive to start)
  • Strong passwords: upper and lower case, phrase, numbers, special characters
  • Data retention policy
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • Retention: task images 3 months; seeker-TukTaker chat history 1 year; emails minimum 10 years; feedback form submissions 5 years; user profiles forever unless the user formally requests removal.
  • Personal data of Seekers and TukTakers is not shared with third parties for advertising or mass marketing.
  • Backup is marked TBC for MVP; backup interval to be agreed.
  • TBC: a chat feature is not described in any flow; NID image retention and encryption; removal-request process.

14. Legal, Compliance & Disclaimers:

  • Hold a company trade licence
  • Comply with VAT and NBR requirements
  • Show disclaimers to users
  • Define Service Seeker and TukTaker terms
Conditions / Business Rules:
  • Spare parts for all works are supplied by the Service Seeker.
  • Both parties take their own safety and security precautions during a task.
  • Seeker releases payment only after accepting satisfactory completion.
  • TukTaker pays own communication costs to and from site.
  • No commission on tips.
  • TukTakers are self-employed contractors, not employees.
  • Customised agreements per service type are future (TBC).
  • 14 further Disclaimer rows in the sheet are blank and need content.

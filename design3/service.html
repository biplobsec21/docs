FAQ

Do I pay anything to post?

No. Posting and receiving offers is free. You pay only when you accept an offer.

What if the TukTaker doesn't show up?

If they don't check in within the agreed time, you can cancel and get a full refund to the account you paid from.

Can I pay in cash?

No. All payments go through Secure Pay so both sides are protected.

What if they damage something?

Raise a dispute before you approve. The payment stays frozen while our team reviews photos from both sides.

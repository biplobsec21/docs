How Sheba's patterns were adapted

  • Sheba sells fixed-price services. TukTakKaj is a bidding marketplace, so the category page shows usual price ranges and “Get offers” in place of Sheba's “Add to cart”.
  • Sheba's checkout became the Post a task page: numbered sections and a sticky summary, with no payment until an offer is accepted.
  • Sheba's order page became job tracking, adding the Secure Pay, arrival code, 48-hour approval and dispute steps.
  • The “Can't find your service?” band leads to posting a task under Other.

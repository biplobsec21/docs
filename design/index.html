How TukTakKaj works

Four steps, and your money never leaves escrow until you say so.

  1. 1

    Post your task

    Pick a category, describe the job, add photos, set the date, place and a budget.

  2. 2

    Compare bids

    NID-verified TukTakers bid. Check ratings, counter-offer up to 3 times, then accept one.

  3. 3

    Pay into escrow

    Pay with bKash, Nagad or Bangla QR within 15 minutes. The money is held, not sent.

  4. 4

    Approve & rate

    When the job is done, approve it. Payment is released and you both rate each other.

Type: Inter (UI) + Hind Siliguri (Bangla). Pill buttons, 12 px cards, soft shadows.

Assumptions shown in the mock

  • Commission 17% + 15% VAT on commission (D28, D29 not final)
  • Payment method chosen at the payment step, not when posting (D11)
  • Pets: No / Cat / Dog / Other (D09) · bidding window 2/6/24/48 h (D12)
  • 48 h to approve, then auto-release (D14) · arrival by GPS + 4-digit code (D17)
  • Hotline number and slogan are placeholders (D42)
